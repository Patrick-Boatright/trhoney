Meet the Beekeeper

I've liked bees for as long as I can remember. When I was little, I'd watch them going from flower to flower, collecting pollen. After high school, I got to talking with a local beekeeper and started learning everything I could. That's when I found out you could actually buy your own honeybees.

I've been keeping bees for four years now, and this has been my best year yet. Even when I'm not working the hives, I still enjoy watching them pollinate the flowers out in my yard.

I'm not in this to get rich. My goal is to share good, local honey at a fair price and keep doing the hobby I love.

What We Believe In

  • Healthy bees come first. We only take what the hive can spare.
  • Small batches, bottled by hand.
  • Honey from our own hives, never blended with outside honey.
  • Selling to neighbors, coworkers and friends in Middle Georgia.

On the Move

Sometimes the bees go for a ride. Hives get moved to better spots for nectar or to split a strong colony into two. Yes, that means a car full of bees, strapped in and closed up tight.
