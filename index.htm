Welcome to TR Honey

TR Honey is a small, local beekeeping operation in Dublin, Georgia. Every jar starts in our own hives, gets pulled by hand, and is bottled in small batches. No big factory, no blending with honey from somewhere else. Just honey from bees that live right here in Middle Georgia.

Most of our customers found us the old fashioned way: a friend handed them a bottle and they came back for more. This site makes it easier to see what we have, learn a little about the bees, and place an order.
